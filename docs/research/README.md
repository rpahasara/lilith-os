# LILITH Research Program

The research program turns difficult architectural questions into falsifiable hypotheses, experiments, and decisions. It is not a parking lot for vague ideas.

## Core artifacts

- [Research Question Register](research-question-register.md) — portfolio of open and evolving questions.
- [Research Method](method.md) — how questions advance and what counts as evidence.

## Historical and evidence reconciliation (2026-09-26)

Documentation/research provenance records. They propose; they do not accept,
renumber, or authorize anything.

- [Source material](source-material/README.md) — archive policy, what is archived and what is still missing.
- [Historical source manifest](source-material/source-manifest.md) — the nine archived originals (S1–S5, Master v1.1, Infrastructure revisions, Engineering Journal) with hashes, dates, classification and supersession.
- [Slice history](slice-history.md) — hierarchy, chronology, and per-slice evidence from the earliest work to 15B2b-B2a.
- [Slice evidence map](slice-evidence-map.md) — slice → hypothesis → evidence → RQs → next boundary, plus a lower-layer dependency trace.
- [Negative results register](negative-results-register.md) — failures, aborted experiments, and open debt, preserved as evidence.
- [RQ numbering reconciliation](rq-numbering-reconciliation.md) — how the REPO and MASTER registers relate; owner decision required.
- [REPO ↔ MASTER crosswalk](rq-register-crosswalk.md) — every REPO:RQ-001–050 and MASTER:RQ-001–121 relationship, classified.
- [RQ evidence map](rq-evidence-map.md) — every in-repo RQ mapped to evidence with a separate, proposed maturity.
- [Research findings](findings/README.md) — detailed, evidence-based finding records.

## Research charters (2026-09-26)

Long-term research doctrine. A charter constrains design and experiments. It
is not an acceptance record, a completed architecture, or proof of
cognition, emotion, or consciousness.

- [Research charters](charters/README.md) — document control, sanitization
  record, links to current slice records, and the C01–C07 ↔ RQ mapping.
- [LILITH Cognitive Continuity, Memory & Substrate Research Charter v0.1](charters/lilith-cognitive-continuity-research-charter-v0.1.md)
  — a cross-slice research and design charter for identity continuity,
  selective memory, substrate replacement, affect, relationship cognition,
  artificial welfare, governance separation, and future multi-runtime work
  ([PDF](charters/LILITH_Cognitive_Continuity_Research_Charter_v0.1.pdf),
  [DOCX](charters/LILITH_Cognitive_Continuity_Research_Charter_v0.1.docx)).

## Program themes

1. Identity and continuity
2. Runtime independence and synchronization
3. Memory, context, and provenance
4. Goals, planning, delegation, and self-evaluation
5. Governance, authority, risk, and emergency behavior
6. Execution, verification, and evidence
7. Proactivity, attention, and human factors
8. Security, privacy, and abuse resistance
9. Evaluation, observability, and regression

Research results should feed architecture, ADRs, tests, roadmap gates, engineering-journal entries, and public writing. Public material must remove personal data, secrets, and operationally sensitive details.
