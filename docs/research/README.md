# LILITH Research Program

The research program turns difficult architectural questions into falsifiable hypotheses, experiments, and decisions. It is not a parking lot for vague ideas.

## Core artifacts

- [Research Question Register](research-question-register.md) — portfolio of open and evolving questions.
- [Research Method](method.md) — how questions advance and what counts as evidence.

## Program themes

1. Identity and continuity
2. Runtime independence and synchronization
3. Memory, context, and provenance
4. Goals, planning, delegation, and self-evaluation
5. Governance, authority, risk, and emergency behavior
6. Execution, verification, and evidence
7. Proactivity, attention, and human factors
8. Security, privacy, and abuse resistance
9. Evaluation, observability, and regression

Research results should feed architecture, ADRs, tests, roadmap gates, engineering-journal entries, and public writing. Public material must remove personal data, secrets, and operationally sensitive details.
