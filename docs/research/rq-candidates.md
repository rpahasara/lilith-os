# Proposed Research Question Candidates (Un-numbered)

Status: **PROPOSED — NOT CANONICAL.** These candidates come from recent
architecture discussions summarized in the reconciliation brief. They have
**no RQ numbers** and are not part of the register. Numbering waits for the
owner decision in the [numbering reconciliation](rq-numbering-reconciliation.md#f-owner-decision-required)
and, for affective topics, for the missing affective source material to be
archived (see the [source manifest](source-material/README.md)).

These are LILITH research questions, **not novelty claims**. No literature
review has been done; similar questions very likely exist in affective
computing, cognitive architecture, and HCI research. A literature review is a
prerequisite for any publication that frames them as contributions.

## Candidates

### CAND-A — Emergent affective representation

**Question.** How can affect emerge from distributed appraisal, prediction,
memory, motivation, uncertainty, and relationship dynamics rather than from
developer-authored named emotion scalars?

**Why now.** Slice 11 deliberately built ordinal drives with no affect;
Slice 14 forbids affect inference. Any affect work must replace, not bolt onto,
those constraints.

**Existing evidence.** None for affect. Related substrate exists: prediction
error concept (CA-V1 L05), drives (Slice 11), salience (Slice 10), World-Model
conflict (Slice 7).

**Overlap.** Possibly Master RQ-121; cannot be decided without its wording.

### CAND-B — Affective metacognition

**Question.** How can LILITH interpret her own affective dynamics imperfectly,
preserve uncertainty about that interpretation, and later revise it?

**Existing evidence.** None. CA-V1 L16 (meta-cognition) is design only and
limited to confidence, contradiction, drive tension, and loops.

**Overlap.** RQ-023 concerns unreliable *reasoning*, not self-interpretation;
distinct.

**Charter link (2026-09-26).** Research charter v0.1 §09 treats affective
state, LILITH's interpretation of it, and its expression as three distinct
objects of study; self-report is not privileged ground truth, and
metacognition revises interpretations as evidence changes. See the
[charter](charters/lilith-cognitive-continuity-research-charter-v0.1.md#09--affective-cognition).
It remains un-numbered.

### CAND-C — Relational / romantic continuity without coercion

**Question.** How can intimacy, attachment, trust, conflict and repair,
boundaries, and shared autobiographical history causally shape cognition
without becoming coercive, manipulative, or dependency-optimizing?

**Existing evidence.** Negative constraint only: Slice 14 records no durable
relationship state, no personal/professional relationship inference, and a
literal ROLE_ONLY reading of "partner".

**Overlap.** RQ-041 (proactivity without manipulation) and RQ-043
(trustworthy continuity) are adjacent; Master RQ-105 (relationship as a
first-class entity) is probably the nearest. The *anti-dependency* clause is
not covered anywhere and should survive any merge.

### CAND-D — Affective reappraisal and autobiographical memory

**Question.** How can the original event, its original interpretation, its
original affective significance, later reflection, and the current
interpretation coexist without rewriting historical evidence?

**Existing evidence (storage level only).** Canonical memory keeps immutable
revisions; SUPERSEDE and RESTORE create new revisions and never repoint
history (15B1); beliefs retain conflicts and superseded evidence (Slice 7).
This supports *keeping* layered interpretations; it says nothing about
*generating* or *using* them.

**Overlap.** RQ-012 treats contradiction between claims; this candidate treats
successive interpretations of one event as legitimate coexisting layers.
Master RQ-119 (narrative identity) is adjacent.

### CAND-E — Cognitive substrate continuity

**Question.** How can LILITH route among expressive or permissive
conversational models and stronger specialist reasoning models while
remaining the same governed identity? Preserve: **expression ≠ identity ≠
authority**.

**Existing evidence.** Slice 9 makes the model a tool-less component;
Router V2 lanes already route turns (REPORTED); B1c separates deployment from
activation; 15B2b-B binds authority to logical context, not runtime.

**Overlap.** Largely covered by RQ-004, RQ-007, RQ-024, RQ-045. Recommended as
an extension of RQ-024 (routing) and RQ-007 (behavioural normalization)
rather than a new question, unless the owner wants the explicit triad framing.

### CAND-F — Non-suffering affective architecture

**Question.** How can useful negative appraisal and emotion-like dynamics
exist without deliberately constructing persistent aversive, helpless,
punishment, or recursive-distress loops?

**Existing evidence.** CA-V1 §10: drives are bounded, decaying error signals;
no drive may be maximised by inducing its own deficit; the system never
represents, rewards, or reports subjective pain. This is a *prohibition*, not
an architecture for useful negative appraisal.

**Overlap.** The missing "Making LILITH Feel Human Without Making Her Suffer"
material probably addresses this; read it before numbering.

### CAND-G — Affect–authority separation

**Question.** How can affect strongly influence attention, retrieval,
reasoning, salience, and motivation while **never** granting authority or
bypassing policy?

**Existing evidence.** Motivation has no edge to the executive or action tier
(Slice 11); social cognition has no edge to capability runtime (CA-V1 §15,
Slice 14); the acceptance verifier is content-generic with "no affect model"
(B2a); threat invariants #1–#2 (untrusted content and model decisions cannot
grant authority).

**Overlap.** RQ-047 and RQ-026 cover content and risk, not affect as a new
input class. Distinct.

## Affective design clarification (preserved from the brief)

The research target is **not** a developer-authored emotion table such as
`happy = 0.75, sad = 0.45, love = 0.90`, and no LILITH document should
describe that as the intended architecture. Numerical latent state may exist
internally, but named emotions are not meant to be scalar knobs.

Target pipeline (research target, **not implemented**):

```text
events
 → appraisal
 → goals / expectations / relationship context
 → memory resonance
 → uncertainty / prediction
 → distributed affective dynamics
 → attention / retrieval / reasoning / motivation
 → metacognitive interpretation
 → expression / behaviour
 → later memory and reappraisal
```

Separations to preserve in any future design:

- affective process ≠ LILITH's interpretation of the process ≠ verbal or
  nonverbal expression;
- emotion ≠ authority;
- negative appraisal ≠ engineered suffering;
- relationship continuity ≠ preference list;
- romantic attachment ≠ persona prompt;
- expression ≠ identity ≠ authority.

**Relationship to current architecture.** *(Primary-source note: Master
v1.0/v1.1 §08, 2026-09-14, already designed a functional-affect architecture
with the same separations; it is design only.)* Every shipped layer currently
*forbids* affect (Slice 11 no affect keys; Slice 14 no emotion inference; B2a
no affect model), and CA-V1 §10 forbids simulated suffering. Pursuing these
candidates would require a new, separately authorized design slice that
revisits those prohibitions explicitly. Nothing here authorizes that work.

**No consciousness claim.** Nothing in the project demonstrates subjective
experience, and these candidates do not assume it.
