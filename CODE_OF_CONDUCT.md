# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, rigorous, and welcoming project. Participants should be able to question assumptions, challenge designs, report safety concerns, and acknowledge uncertainty without hostility or ridicule.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, consent, and security boundaries.
- Give appropriate credit for ideas and work.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized or demeaning conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe and productive community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner. A dedicated contact and fuller enforcement procedure will be published before the project accepts a broad contributor community.
