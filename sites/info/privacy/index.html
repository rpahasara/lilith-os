Privacy Policy

How LILITH handles your data

Effective date: 18 November 2026

1. About LILITH

LILITH is an independently operated personal AI companion and operating system, intended for authorized private use by its operator. It is not a company and is not offered to the public: there are no sign-ups and no other users.

Privacy questions and requests: ravindupahasara2@gmail.com.

2. Google data LILITH accesses

LILITH connects only to the operator's own Google accounts, with these read-only permissions:

PermissionWhat LILITH reads
gmail.readonlyFor recent messages: sender, recipients, subject, date, Gmail's short text preview (“snippet”), and message and thread identifiers. LILITH's automations do not download full message bodies or attachments; in a conversation, LILITH can open a specific message when the operator asks.
calendar.calendarlist.readonlyThe list of the operator's calendars.
calendar.events.readonlyUpcoming events: title, description, location, start and end, attendees, video-call link and status.

LILITH never sends, modifies or deletes email, and never creates, changes or deletes calendar events.

3. How the data is used

  • Important-mail alerts: recent messages are checked for urgency, and the operator is notified about important ones.
  • Job-application tracking: career-related messages update a personal record of applications (company, role, stage, contacts and activity). LILITH also keeps its own working notes (“beliefs”) derived from that record.
  • Meeting preparation: before a meeting, related recent messages are gathered into a short brief.
  • Daily brief and agenda: the day's events and recent career-related mail are summarised for the operator.
  • Conversations: when the operator asks LILITH about these things, the relevant information can be used to answer.

Google data is used only to provide these features to the operator. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with data brokers.

4. AI processing by third parties

LILITH uses external AI services to read, classify and summarise. Message headers and snippets, and event details, are sent to them only to perform the tasks above:

  • OpenAI, through the operator's own ChatGPT account, for all of LILITH's automatic email and calendar tasks and for most conversations.
  • In conversations only: Groq for some replies, GitHub Copilot as a backup model, and ElevenLabs when a reply is spoken aloud.

Each provider processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy, including its own rules on retention and model training.

5. Delivery and hosting

  • Discord and Telegram: alerts, briefs and LILITH's replies reach the operator through these messaging services, so their content (which can include senders and subjects) passes through them.
  • Hosting: LILITH runs on a private server rented from a hosting provider.
  • Cloudflare: LILITH's private web dashboard is served through Cloudflare, which also limits access to the operator.

6. Storage and retention

  • Application records (job applications with contact names and email addresses, activity titles and notes, and message identifiers) and the beliefs derived from them are kept until they are deleted manually.
  • Working files (urgent-mail candidates, meeting context and the agenda) are replaced as LILITH runs.
  • Logs: some application logs can contain subject lines and email addresses and are kept until deleted manually. System logs are limited in size and the oldest entries are removed automatically.
  • Assistant history: LILITH's conversation and task history can contain information derived from Google data and is kept until deleted manually.
  • Backups: application information may be included in protected backups, both on the server and in encrypted offline copies. Backups can keep copies after the information has been deleted from the active application.

LILITH does not currently delete this information automatically or after a fixed period.

7. Sharing

Google data is not sold, rented or given to anyone except the service providers in sections 4 and 5, and only as needed to provide LILITH's features to the operator. No person other than the operator reads it.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

LILITH's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Security

The dashboard is reachable only by the operator through Cloudflare Access. Google access credentials are stored on the server with owner-only permissions and are never published. Connections to Google, Cloudflare and the AI providers are encrypted in transit.

10. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. LILITH then can no longer read Gmail or Calendar. Revoking access does not delete information already stored.
  • Stored information is deleted manually on request. Copies in backups remain until those backups are replaced or deleted.
  • If your name, email address or messages appear in the operator's mail and you want that information removed from LILITH, write to the address in section 1.

11. Children

LILITH is not directed at children and has no users other than its operator.

12. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
