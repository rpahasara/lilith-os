LILITH

LILITH is an independently operated personal AI companion and operating system, intended for authorized private use. It is not a company or a public service: there are no sign-ups, and the application itself is available only to its operator.

What LILITH does

LILITH helps its operator keep track of their day: it notices important email, keeps a personal record of job applications, prepares short briefs before meetings and assembles a daily summary of the schedule.

How LILITH uses Google

With its operator's permission, LILITH connects to the operator's own Google accounts with read-only access:

Gmail — read only

Reads the sender, recipients, subject, date and Gmail's short preview of recent messages, to flag urgent mail, update the job-application record and gather context for meetings. LILITH never sends, changes or deletes email.

Google Calendar — read only

Lists the operator's calendars and reads upcoming events, to show the agenda and prepare meeting briefs. LILITH never creates, changes or deletes events.

Only the operator's own accounts are connected. Some of this information is processed by external AI services. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is read, who processes it, where it is stored, and how access can be revoked.
